How it works

Same game file: everyone needs the same copy of Blockburg (easiest: put it online and share the link).

The host runs the world. If the host leaves, the game ends for everyone.

Up to 8 players. Works best on home internet; some school or work networks block it.

Your look (hat, shirt, pants, face, colors) comes with you.
